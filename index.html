Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

How long does it take to build custom software?
Depending on the complexity, a standard web application takes 4-8 weeks. We provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.
Do you offer support after the system is deployed?
Absolutely! We provide 24/7 technical support, regular updates, and maintenance packages to ensure your system never goes down.
Can the software work offline?
Yes, our POS and inventory systems are designed to work offline and automatically sync to the cloud once an internet connection is restored.
How much does it cost?
We offer flexible pricing based on your needs. Whether you are a startup or a large enterprise, we have packages that fit your budget. Contact us for a free quote!
